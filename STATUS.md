**👉 2026-07-30: Home's rails, rotating stats, a 4-colour facet palette, the item-detail rebuild, unrate, and a perf audit.** Per [.claude/plans/ethereal-wiggling-dijkstra.md](.claude/plans/ethereal-wiggling-dijkstra.md) (IDs `R1`–`R10` in TASKS.md).
