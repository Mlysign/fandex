Rating prompt

Google Play's own review sheet, so someone can rate Fandex on the store without leaving the app.

When it shows
Once, right after a person's tenth rating has saved.
When it never shows
After a rating that failed to save, or after any error on that screen. Never a second time.
What Google decides
Google may skip it, and it never tells the app whether the person rated. So the app counts "shown" as "answered" and doesn't ask again.
What we can't change
The look and the words. They follow the phone's language and its light or dark setting.

Build it like this?

  • A. Once, right after the tenth rating saves. My pick
  • B. Once, after the tenth rating and no sooner than a week after install. Fewer people asked, but the ones asked have stayed.
